<p><strong style="color:#1f5fa8;">Streamline</strong>: a line everywhere tangent to the velocity vector at a given instant.</p>

<p><strong style="color:#c0392b;">Streakline</strong>: the locus of particles that have earlier passed through a prescribed point.</p>

<p><strong style="color:#1e8449;">Pathline</strong>: the actual path traversed by a given fluid particle.</p>
<hr>

<p><strong>Flow model (estimation):</strong> steady potential flow past a cylinder. The unsteady flow is <strong>“modelled”</strong> by adding an oscillating circulation and a street of point vortices, shed alternately and carried downstream. The streamline is traced through the velocity field at one instant, the streakline joins particles released continuously from P, and the pathline follows one particle over time.</p>


**Disclaimer:** the flow shown is only for demonstration purpuses, I am using the term <strong>"estimation"</strong> and <strong>"modelled"</strong> at the looses sense possible.
