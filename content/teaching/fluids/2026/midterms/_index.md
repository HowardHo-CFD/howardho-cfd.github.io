But in the mean time, here's some tips on how to prep for midterms in general:

Typically, I suggest the order of tutorial questions -> textbook problem sets -> past midterm papers. You want to make sure all the tutorial questions made sense first, since they're often quite fundamental. For textbook problems, try to solve questions that look less common/different to the ones you've seen before, this way you get better coverage. The course main Quercus page should have some past paper examples for reference/practice. I personally save past papers last as they are very finite, and I suggest doing them in exam like conditions (e.g. start a timer, have the official crib sheet out only).
